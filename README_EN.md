# Hemp-Bio-DropStitch: Biodegradable Hemp Inflatable Drop-Stitch Composite

**Project Status:** Public Domain (CC0 1.0 Universal).  
This technology is open-source and free for global manufacturing, modification, and commercial use without patent restrictions.

---

## Engineering Concept

**Hemp-Bio-DropStitch** is a roll-to-roll biodegradable composite fabric with internal drop-stitch spacer yarns designed for high-pressure inflatable structural panels (e.g., 3×3×3 m modular shelters, flooring, pontoons) with **controlled biodegradation**. It is manufactured on standard SUP-fabric production lines (double-needle-bar Raschel machine + washing line + calender) without hardware modification and decomposes cleanly in soil/compost at the end of its service life.

---

## 1. Manufacturing Process & Technical Specifications (Factory RFQ)

* **Reinforcement & Drop-Stitch Yarns:** High-twist combed hemp yarn with a spacer yarn density of **20–24 threads/cm²**. Ensures flat panel geometry without ballooning at working pressures of **6–10 PSI** (0.4–0.7 bar).
* **Yarn Preparation (Sizing & Desizing):** Prior to knitting on a double-needle-bar Raschel machine, hemp yarn is treated with a **water-soluble starch bio-sizing** to eliminate fuzz and reduce needle friction. After knitting, the greige fabric undergoes standard warm-water washing with natural surfactants/enzymes to completely remove sizing prior to lamination.
* **Internal Bio-Protection:** Natural antiseptics (tannins / bark extract) are introduced during the final rinse to prevent internal fiber mildew from trapped condensation during service life.
* **Hermetic Lamination Layer:** Compostable flexible bio-thermoplastic **PBAT / PHBH** (certified *OK Compost HOME*). Applied via hot calendering within a processing temperature window of **140–165 °C**, ensuring deep mechanical adhesion without thermal embrittlement of the hemp fibers.
* **Seam-Welding Tape Material:** Smooth hemp fabric (without spacer yarns) laminated on the same calender with the same **PBAT / PHBH** compound, slit into strips for airtight hot-air welding (180–220 °C) or HF welding of panel edges and openings.

---

## 2. Key Performance & Lifecycle Properties

1. **Durability & Weather Resistance:** Outer hemp weave shields the bio-polymer from UV degradation, stays flexible in sub-zero temperatures, and withstands repeated rolling for compact transport.
2. **Standard Assembly:** Unlike vulcanized natural rubber (latex), the PBAT/PHBH layer is a true thermoplastic, enabling fast, monolithic seam welding with standard PVC/TPU hot-air equipment.
3. **Controlled End-of-Life Biodegradation:** Remains stable for years while inflated and ventilated. Once decommissioned, cut panels buried in moist soil or compost are completely digested by microorganisms into humus within **6–18 months** with zero microplastic residue.

## 3. Order Quantities (R&D & Pilot Run)

* **R&D Sample Run:** 100–200 m² roll (main drop-stitch fabric + matching seam-welding tape).
* **Initial Commercial Batch:** 3,000–5,000 m² (approx. 50–80 modular 3×3×3 m units).
